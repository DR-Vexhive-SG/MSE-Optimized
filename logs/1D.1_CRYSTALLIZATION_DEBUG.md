## Root Cause Identified

**Problem:** The 40 crystallized patterns reported in 1C.17 are counted DURING backtest (in-memory), but the save happens AFTER. However, only 4 are in DB after reload.

**Most Likely Cause:** The 36 "missing" crystallized patterns are:
1. NOT newly crystallized during 1C.17
2. BUT were crystallized in PREVIOUS runs (1C.10-1C.16)
3. Those runs had the KeyError bug (strategy case mismatch)
4. Patterns from broken runs were never saved
5. 1C.17 only crystallized 4 NEW patterns (which WERE saved)

**Evidence:**
- 1C.10-1C.16: Backtest failed with KeyError, patterns not saved
- 1C.17: First successful backtest with all fixes
- Only 4 patterns crystallized in 1C.17 (real number)
- 40 reported was CUMULATIVE (including lost patterns from broken runs)

**Fix:**
1. Run 3-5 consecutive backtests with 1C.17 fixes
2. Patterns will accumulate properly
3. Should reach 40+ crystallized after 3-5 runs
